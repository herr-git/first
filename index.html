What do these words mean?
Price
The cost of one token or one fund share, in US dollars.
Market value
For a token: price times the number of tokens in circulation. For a fund: the total value of everything the fund holds (its total assets).
24-hour volume
How much was traded in the last 24 hours. Crypto trades all day, every day.
Volume today
How many fund shares changed hands today. Funds only trade during US stock market hours (9:30 to 16:00 New York time, weekdays).
Stablecoin
A token designed to stay at a fixed price, usually 1 US dollar.
Bid / ask
Bid is the highest price a buyer is offering right now. Ask is the lowest price a seller will accept.
Yearly fee
What the fund charges each year, as a percentage of the money invested.
Delayed 15 minutes
Free data shows prices from about 15 minutes ago, not this second.
Sample data
Made-up numbers used to show the layout. They are not real prices.
