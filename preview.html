  • The mix is fixed: it starts with the percentages you chose and is never adjusted afterwards (no "rebalancing").
  • Biggest drop means the largest fall from a high point to a later low point during the 6 months.
  • A calm month may show little or no drop. That does not mean the mix is safe.
  • Funds do not trade at weekends or on US holidays. On those days the last closing price is carried forward.
  • Token prices are CoinGecko's daily price at midnight UTC. Fund prices are the US closing price (4 pm New York time). So token and fund prices for the same day are taken a few hours apart.
  • Your mix is not saved in this preview. It is gone when you leave the page.
